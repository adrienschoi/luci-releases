# Luci for Mac

**Notice when your focus slips, while there's still time to get it back.**

Luci is a menu bar app for work and study sessions. It notices when you start working, stays quiet while you're focused, and sends one short, tappable heads-up when your attention drifts.

> *You were working on the report, now on YouTube for 15 min. Head back to it?*

No to-do lists, no scores, no streaks.

## What to expect

- **Polite:** never interrupts while you're typing or in a meeting, and leaves at least 10 minutes between nudges.
- **Tap-only:** every question is answered with a tap.
- **Private:** sees app and website names and how long you spent in them, never the words on your screen.
- **Learns you:** picks up your working style over time, including which apps are work for you and when a reminder actually helps.

Luci is invite-only for now. You'll need the code from your invitation.

## Install

1. Download **Luci-x.y.dmg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/adrienschoi/luci-releases/releases/latest) (under *Assets*).
2. Drag **Luci** to **Applications**, open it, and enter your invite code.
3. Allow the permissions it asks for.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. Works on Intel and Apple silicon Macs.

## Update

Download the newest DMG and replace the old copy in Applications. Your history and settings stay.
